> **Status:** placeholder. To be written by the team. Until then, follow the basics below and the "Content" sections in component docs.

## Basics (in use in the reference screens)
- Sentence case for titles, labels and buttons ("Link your bank account", "Continue"). Title Case only for tab names.
- Titles ask or instruct ("What's your mobile number?"); descriptions say why in one line ("We'll send a 4-digit OTP to verify it.").
- Buttons are verbs. No "Click here", no "Submit" when a specific verb exists.
- Money: `₹` + Indian grouping (₹1,84,250.75). Changes always signed (+1.09%, −0.42%).
- Plain words over jargon. When jargon is unavoidable (XIRR, LTP), explain it in a Tooltip.
